# Changelog

Each section is a version of the mod, the `version` in `mod.json`. The release workflow copies the
section of a tagged version into the release notes.

## 1.1.0

- A character's own gear that a chest also brings back, though the Trivia page does not list it:
  Rick's By Road and Leazas Red General Armor, Kouhime's Poison Dango and Crane's Stealth Suit. It is
  lost while its owner is recruited and no copy is owned, the chest's own rule (there is no counter).
  Its row in the choice says whose it is and how it comes back: as it was, or with the world's mark
  in the 2nd and 3rd worlds (the By Road and the Poison Dango).

## 1.0.1

- Needs loader 2.56.0. With 13 or more lost Rare Items, the choice of the item ran past the bottom of
  the screen, and its last rows and "More..." could not be clicked. Loader 2.56.0 moves a menu that
  long up until it fits.

## 1.0.0

First version.
- Pluepet Rare Finds on Frostbuy's Items and Other rack, 50,000 Gold, while a Rare Item is lost; one
  contract at a time; the purchase refused when nothing is lost.
- Pluepet's scene over the shop: the lost item chosen from the game's menu, Rance's lines the first
  time, an owner's line once per item.
- The receipt in the vault with the item tracked and the quests left; it cannot be sold or donated.
- After 3 successful exploration quests: the delivery in Rance's Room with the game's item moment,
  then the game's autosave. An item found before the delivery: another one, or the money back.
